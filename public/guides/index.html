Guides for work that needs your attention

Six practical guides to the methods behind DeepWork: the Pomodoro Technique, time blocking, the Eisenhower matrix, habit streaks, a workable daily routine, and how to choose a focus timer you actually own. No growth hacks, no ten-step morning routines, no invented research.

Every guide is written by Rajat Malik, the developer of DeepWork, and is checked against the app's own behaviour. The six articles carry advertising; the editorial policy explains how that is kept separate from what gets published.

How these guides are written

Each guide starts from the method, not from the product. DeepWork appears where it genuinely implements the idea — a configurable timer, a day planner, the matrix, streaks — and the guide says so. Where the evidence for a technique is thin, the guide says that too. Nothing here is a paid placement, and no guide contains a testimonial the project cannot show you.

The app itself is free and open source under the MIT licence. It keeps its database on your machine, and it does not load advertising: the ad code on these guides is confined to the guides. More about DeepWork.

Practise the method in the app

DeepWork is a focus timer, task manager and habit tracker with no account and no cloud. Open it in your browser, or install the version for Windows, macOS or Linux.
