Advertising policy

Effective 17 November 2026 · Last updated 17 November 2026

This page states where advertising appears on this site, how it is placed, and the rules the site follows. It is written against Google's AdSense Program policies, Google Publisher Policies and ad placement policies.

1. Where advertising appears

Advertising is served by Google AdSense and appears only on the six written guide articles listed below:

  • The Pomodoro Technique
  • Time blocking
  • The Eisenhower matrix
  • A daily deep work routine
  • Habit streaks
  • Choosing a focus timer

There is no ad code on:

  • the application interface, including the dashboard, tasks, matrix, calendar, habits, journal, analytics, settings and reminders;
  • the guide index at /guides/, which exists for navigation between articles;
  • this page, the About page, the editorial policy, the privacy policy, the terms or the contact page.

The application interface does not load ad code: it is a desktop and web tool, not an ad-supported screen. Links from it to these guides open in the user's own browser. That keeps the site clear of Google's rule against placing ads in software applications, in private communications, in emails, in pop-ups, or in framed content.

2. How ads are placed

The guides use the standard, unmodified asynchronous AdSense tag. Advertising is placed in manual in-article units, not Auto ads, so the position of every unit is chosen and can be checked. A guide carries at most two display units, both inside the article body and below the opening section.

  • No unit is placed above the first paragraph or in the header, footer or navigation.
  • No unit is placed next to a download, play, next/previous or other action button.
  • No unit is placed inside the call-to-action band at the end of a guide.
  • No ad is placed in an iframe or in framed content of any kind.
  • Each unit is separated from the text above and below it and is labelled “Advertisement”.
  • There are no arrows, flashy animations or images pointing at an ad.
  • Content is never formatted to look like an ad, and an ad is never formatted to look like content or navigation.
  • Ads do not auto-refresh, auto-redirect, overlap content, cover content or appear before the content can be read.
  • There are no pop-ups, pop-unders, interstitials or new-window ad clicks.

The site does not use “Sponsored Links” or “Advertisements” in a misleading way, and does not use labels such as “Resources”, “Helpful links” or “Today's top offers” above an ad.

3. No incentivised or invalid traffic

Invalid clicks and impressions are prohibited. Clicks and impressions must come from genuine interest. The site does not ask anyone to click an ad, does not offer compensation or rewards for viewing or clicking an ad, and does not use arrows, misleading images or language such as “support us” to draw attention to one.

  • The developer does not click the site's own ads, on any device, for any reason.
  • No automated clicking, impression-generating, paid-to-click, paid-to-surf, autosurf, click-exchange or traffic-exchange service is used.
  • No traffic is purchased. The site is not promoted through unsolicited mass email or unwanted third-party placements.
  • No software, toolbar or browser extension loads these pages or triggers their ads.
  • Traffic sources are reviewed in analytics; unusual activity is investigated rather than encouraged.

ads.txt lists Google as the authorised seller (pub-4991682021863699). No other ad system is authorised to sell this inventory.

4. Content standards

The guides are original, written and reviewed by the developer, and each one has a real editorial purpose. They are not pages published only to show ads, and the site does not carry more advertising than content.

  • No illegal, hateful, harassing, sexually explicit, violent, weapons, tobacco, drug, gambling, malware or deceptive content.
  • No misleading health, financial, political or scientific claims; no fabricated statistics, reviews, ratings or testimonials.
  • No replicated or scraped content, and no “doorway” pages created for search engines.
  • No user-generated content visible to other users. The contact form is private correspondence, not a public comment area; the application's journal and tasks stay on the user's own device.
  • The site is a general-audience productivity site. It is not directed to children under 13 and is not tagged for child-directed ad treatment.
  • No personalised advertising is selected or targeted using sensitive categories, children's data, health data, financial status, ethnicity, religion, criminal records, political affiliation, union membership or sexual orientation.

5. Privacy and consent

The privacy policy discloses the cookies, web beacons, IP addresses and identifiers used as a consequence of Google's ad serving, names Google as a third-party vendor, and explains how to opt out of personalised advertising. It also links to How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps.

Where consent is required — in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and Brazil — a Google-certified consent message is used before personalised advertising cookies are set. The site does not pass personal information to Google's ad systems, does not upload customer lists, and does not attempt to identify users or merge identities.

6. Technical and content-access rules

  • Googlebot and Mediapartners-Google are allowed by robots.txt; the ad pages are not blocked from crawling and are not behind a login.
  • The ad pages answer with a real 200 status and are static HTML, so their content can be evaluated.
  • There are no pop-ups, auto-redirects, forced downloads, malware or unwanted software.
  • The site follows the Better Ads Standards: no intrusive interstitials, no autoplaying sound, no large sticky ads.
  • The AdSense tag is the standard snippet from Google; it is not hidden, altered to inflate performance, or made to cover content.
  • The application is built with Tauri and Angular; its installers are unsigned, which is disclosed on the About page. It contains no ad code and no ad network SDK.

7. Reporting an ad or a policy problem

If an ad on a guide is inappropriate, use the AdChoices icon on the ad to report it to Google, or write to contact@rajatmalik.dev with the page address and a screenshot. Reports about the site's own content, a broken page or a policy concern are read and answered by the developer.

Google's own reporting routes are linked from the AdSense policy FAQs. To report a site that may be violating Google's policies, use the “Ads by Google” or “AdChoices” label on one of its ads.

8. Changes

This policy is reviewed when the ad setup, the guides or Google's policies change. The date at the top moves with a substantive change. It does not replace Google's policies; where this page and Google's policies differ, Google's policies govern.

9. Contact

Questions about advertising on this site can go to contact@rajatmalik.dev. The editorial policy explains how the guides are written, and the privacy policy explains cookies and consent.
