Contact

Talk to the person who builds it

DeepWork is a personal project, not a company with a support desk. Messages go to Rajat, who reads and answers them himself, usually within one business day.

Support and bug reports

For a problem with the app, write to support@rajatmalik.dev. If the app is still running, Settings → Copy diagnostics produces the version, platform and recent log lines; including that saves a round trip. The GitHub issue tracker is also open, and is the better place for a reproducible bug that other people may hit.

Corrections to a guide

If a guide is wrong, out of date or unclear, write to contact@rajatmalik.dev with the page address and the sentence in question. Corrections are published with the update date changed. The editorial policy explains how that works.

Privacy and data requests

For a question about the privacy policy, or a request to access or delete correspondence, use contact@rajatmalik.dev. The developer cannot access or delete data stored inside your copy of the app; that data is yours and stays on your device.

Advertising and business

The guides carry advertising served by Google AdSense. Ad selection, pricing and payment are handled by Google; the developer does not sell placements, and no advertiser can pay for a mention in a guide. For anything else, write to contact@rajatmalik.dev.

Use the form inside the app

If you already have DeepWork open, its Contact page has a form. It posts through Web3Forms and asks for a name, an email address and a message.

Response time. Usually within one business day. There is no guaranteed support SLA, and the project has no obligation to implement a request.
