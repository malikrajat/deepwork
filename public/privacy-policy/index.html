Privacy policy

Effective 17 November 2026 · Last updated 17 November 2026

The short version. The DeepWork application keeps your tasks, focus sessions, habits and journal on your own device. There is no DeepWork account and no backend that receives them. This website uses analytics to count visits and, on the guide pages only, Google AdSense to show advertising. The sections below describe each of those in detail, including the cookies involved and how to refuse them.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller for this website and the DeepWork application is Rajat Malik, an individual developer based in India. Questions, requests and complaints can be sent to contact@rajatmalik.dev. This policy covers app.deepwork.rajatmalik.dev and the DeepWork application distributed from it and from GitHub Releases.

2. What the application stores on your device

DeepWork is local-first. Everything you create is stored on the device you run it on, in a database the app reads and writes directly:

  • Tasks, including deadlines, priorities and recurrence rules.
  • Focus sessions, timer history and time-by-task records.
  • Habits and their completion history.
  • Journal entries and any formatting they contain.
  • Preferences such as timer lengths, theme, reminder schedules and notification settings.
  • Statistics for the optional visitor counter, and the last update check.

On the desktop builds this is a SQLite database file in your operating system's application-data folder. In the browser build it is the browser's localStorage for this origin. It is not a cookie, it is not transmitted to this website, and it is not readable by another site.

You can export a JSON backup, import one, and delete records from inside the app. Removing the app, clearing the browser's site data or deleting the database file removes the records permanently. DeepWork cannot restore data it never had a copy of.

3. What this website collects

The application is a static site. There is no login, no server-side account and no user-generated content stored on a server. The following third-party services are involved when you visit.

Hosting and delivery

The site is published with GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare. Both providers process standard request data — IP address, requested URL, user agent, timestamps — to serve the pages, protect the service and keep security logs. That processing is governed by GitHub's privacy statement and Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Visit counting and analytics

The application page loads Google Tag Manager container GTM-PNJRG4WR, which loads Google Analytics 4 property G-L6WB5FHKPQ. It records page views, sessions, approximate location derived from IP address, device and browser information, and interaction events such as scrolls, outbound clicks, downloads and form interactions. Email addresses in event data are redacted by the container's configuration, Google Signals is disallowed, and Google Analytics 4 does not store full IP addresses.

The web application also uses a first-party GoatCounter counter to answer "is anyone using this". It is cookie-less, does not store IP addresses, and can be turned off in the app's Settings. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are honoured by that counter.

Cloudflare Web Analytics is injected by the host. It is cookie-less and reports aggregate page views and performance measurements, not individual visitors.

The application interface, the guide index and the policy pages do not load ad code, and no task, focus session, habit or journal entry is ever sent to an advertiser. The site has no public user-generated content: no comments, forums, profiles or reviews visible to other visitors. Contact-form messages are private correspondence. The application's tasks, habits and journal entries stay on the user's own device.

6. The contact form and email

The in-app contact form posts to Web3Forms, an email relay that forwards your name, email address and message to the developer's inbox. It also uses hCaptcha to reduce spam; hCaptcha receives the information needed to distinguish a person from a bot, under its own privacy policy. If you write by email instead, your address and message are handled by the email provider.

Messages are used only to read and answer you. They are not added to a mailing list, sold or shared, except where the law requires it. You can ask for the correspondence to be deleted at any time.

7. Legal bases for processing

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, the bases are:

  • Consent — for analytics and advertising cookies where consent is required, and for the optional visitor counter. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests — keeping the site secure, preventing abuse, understanding aggregate usage, and answering messages you choose to send.
  • Legal obligation — retaining information where the law requires it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. To exercise any of these, write to contact@rajatmalik.dev.

California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, to delete it, to correct it, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell personal information. To opt out of advertising cookies, use the controls in section 5.

Brazil (LGPD). If you are in Brazil, the Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD) gives you the right to confirm whether we process your data, to access it, to correct incomplete or inaccurate data, to anonymise, block or delete unnecessary data, to data portability, to information about sharing, and to withdraw consent. You can exercise these rights by writing to contact@rajatmalik.dev. You may also contact the Brazilian National Data Protection Authority (ANPD). The data controller is Rajat Malik, India; the legal bases are consent for advertising and analytics cookies and legitimate interests for security and answering messages. International transfers rely on the safeguards described in the providers' policies.

Because the application stores its data on your device, the developer cannot access, export or delete your tasks, sessions or journal entries. You can do that from inside the app.

9. Data retention

Analytics reports are retained according to the settings of the Google Analytics property and GoatCounter account, typically for a limited period. Advertising data is retained by Google under its own policies. Contact messages are kept only as long as needed to answer them, unless you ask for deletion sooner.

10. International transfers

GitHub, Cloudflare, Google, Web3Forms and hCaptcha are based in or operate from the United States and other countries. Their processing may involve transferring data outside your country, under the safeguards described in their own privacy policies.

11. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. The application keeps its database on your device rather than on a server, which removes a whole class of remote breach. That does not make the local copy invulnerable: the security of your records depends on the security of your device and on whether you keep backups.

12. Children and teens

DeepWork is a productivity tool for general use and is not directed at children under 13. It is not tagged for child-directed ad treatment, and we do not use interest-based advertising or remarketing based on activity by users known to be under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information, contact the address above and it will be deleted.

Where local law gives extra protections to teens — for example the Ad-serving Protections for Teens — Google applies its own safeguards to the ad request. We do not target ads using age, gender or other sensitive categories.

13. Changes to this policy

If the way DeepWork handles data changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top changed. Material changes — for example, adding a new advertising partner — will be described here rather than quietly applied.

14. Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request about your data, can go to contact@rajatmalik.dev. The terms of use and the editorial policy are the companion documents.
